Privacy

Plain-language summary of what this website and the Mark LIV app do with your data.

Last updated: September 24, 2026 · Published by Your Name

The short version

The app keeps your data on your computer. The one thing that leaves it is your conversation with Google’s Gemini API while a session is open. This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

This website

  • No tracking. We don’t run analytics, set cookies or load third-party fonts, scripts or ads.
  • Hosting logs. Whoever hosts the site (for example GitHub Pages, Netlify or Cloudflare) may keep standard server logs such as IP address and page requested. That is governed by the host’s own policy.
  • Download pages ask GitHub’s public API for the latest version number and file sizes. Your browser contacts GitHub when you view them.
  • Contact form. What you type (name, email, message) is used only to reply to you. It is sent to the form service configured by the site owner, or opens in your own email app.

The Mark LIV app

  • Stored on your computer only: your API key and settings, dashboard accounts (email and a salted password hash), the dashboard’s TLS certificate, and the assistant’s memory of you. See the data table. There is no Mark LIV server and no telemetry.
  • Sent to Google (Gemini API): your voice audio and text while a session is open, the assistant’s replies, and any screen capture or webcam frame you ask it to look at. Google processes this under its own terms and privacy policy for the Gemini API. Nothing is streamed while you are muted, while it is asleep, or when the app is closed.
  • Sent to other services when you use a skill: for example web searches go to the search provider, weather and flight lookups to their data sources, and messages to the messaging app you choose.
  • Phone dashboard: works over your local network. Commands are AES-256 encrypted between the phone and your computer; the connection also uses a self-signed HTTPS certificate created on your machine. If you enable Google sign-in, your browser talks to Google to sign you in and your computer verifies Google’s response; it stores your email, name and profile picture URL locally.

Your choices

  • Delete memory/long_term.json (or use the memory panel) to make the assistant forget you.
  • Delete config/accounts.json to remove all dashboard accounts.
  • Revoke or regenerate your Gemini API key at aistudio.google.com at any time.

Children

The assistant is not directed at children under 13, and Google’s Gemini terms set their own age requirements.
